Getting games

The Retro Shelf doesn't come with games, and it doesn't point to sites that hand out copies of commercial games. Downloading those is against the law in most countries even if you own the original, because the copy you download isn't yours. These are the ways to do it right.

Copy games you own

CartridgesPlug a cartridge into a reader and copy it to your computer, then drag the file onto the shelf. Readers exist for NES, SNES, N64, Genesis, Game Boy and GBA.
GB OperatorRetrodeOpen Source Cartridge Reader
DiscsPlayStation, PS2, PSP and Dreamcast discs can be copied with a computer's disc drive or the console itself; GameCube and Wii discs with a Wii.
Dumping guidesCleanRip for Wii

Free games, made to be shared

HomebrewNew games for old consoles, given away by the people who made them. Download, then drag onto the shelf.
Homebrew Hub (Game Boy)PDRomsitch.io

Buy them again

Official re-releasesMany classics are sold again for modern computers and consoles. These play in their own apps, not here.
GOGEvercadeLimited Run Games

Rename

The name on the box and spine. Leave it empty to go back to the original.

Welcome to The Retro Shelf

  1. Add your games. Drag game files onto this window, or press Add games. Each one goes on the right shelf, cover and all.
  2. Pick one and play. Arrow keys or a controller to browse, Enter or A to play. In a game, hold Start or press Esc to come back here.
  3. Turn a box over with Space or Y to see when you last played, your saves and more. Right-click a box to rename it or change its cover.
