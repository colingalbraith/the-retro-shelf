Welcome to The Retro Shelf

  1. Add your games. Drag them onto this window or press Add games. They get sorted by console, and the box art is found for you.
  2. Play. Browse with the arrow keys or a controller, then press Enter or A. To get back here from a game, hold Start or press Esc.
  3. Turn a box over with Space or Y to see when you last played and your saves. Press O, Select on a controller, or right-click for more.
  4. Look around. Collections show the series your games belong to, and Your play keeps your hours and pins. Both are in the View menu.
